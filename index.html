Pet Merge 2048 · Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short version: Pet Merge 2048 is a completely offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDKs. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Pet Merge 2048 ("the App") is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, contact us at ran@example.com (replace with your real email before publishing).

2. What data we collect

No personal data is collected. Specifically:

  • No account, no sign-in. You never create an account or provide an email inside the App.
  • No internet permission. The Android app declares no INTERNET permission and makes no network requests.
  • No analytics or tracking. No advertising IDs, no device identifiers, no usage tracking, no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files. The App requests no such permissions.
  • No ads and no third-party SDKs. The App is a self-contained WebView with no external libraries.

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally using the browser's built-in localStorage mechanism inside the App's private WebView storage. This includes:

  • unlocked levels, stars, and cleared levels
  • your pet collection and achievements
  • high scores and local leaderboard entries (score, pet reached, moves, date)
  • daily-quest status and audio on/off preferences
  • tutorial completion status

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, synced, or shared with us or anyone else. Only you can see it, on the device where you play.

4. How to delete your data

You can remove all locally stored progress at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings (Android Settings → Apps → Pet Merge 2048 → Storage → Clear Data), or by uninstalling the App. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

5. Children's privacy

The App is rated for Everyone and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. There is no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links.

6. Permissions

The App requires no dangerous permissions and no network access. It does not request storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or phone permissions.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since the App collects no data, material changes are unlikely.
